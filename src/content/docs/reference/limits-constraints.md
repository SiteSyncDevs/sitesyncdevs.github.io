As of 1.0.0 (see the [release notes](/release-notes/1-0-0/)):

- Gateway-level health metrics are not shown on site or Enterprise dashboards.
- Never-activated timestamps, decoder-state logic, and very old Last Seen values need additional validation.
- Additional layout work remains for tag-path management in Standard projects.
