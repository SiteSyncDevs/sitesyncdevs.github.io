SiteSync Enterprise is the multi-site offering. It supports many sites while letting a central enterprise team manage the administrative side — credentials, connections, decoders, device profiles — and exposing only device onboarding and management to site users.

It's made of related deliverables, not one interface:

- **[Enterprise Management](/products/enterprise-management/overview/)** — the central admin app.
- **[Field App](/products/field-app/overview/)** — the site-level app (browser or Perspective mobile).

Both inherit from the parent **SiteSync Source** project.
