Access is governed by your Ignition Identity Provider. See [Roles and permissions](/getting-started/roles-and-permissions/) for the full breakdown.

- **Administrator** — manages Enterprise Management (connections, profiles, decoders, sites, fleet); enterprise-wide visibility.
- **General (field user)** — signs in to the Field App; scoped to their assigned site.

Connection credentials configured in Enterprise Management are never exposed to Field App users.
