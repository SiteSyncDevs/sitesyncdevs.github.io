The Field App centers on three things: choosing your **site**, working with **Devices**, and opening a **device page**.

- **Site selection** — choose your site on sign-in. See [Select a site](/use/field-app/select-site/).
- **Devices** — search or scan to find a device. See [Find and open a device](/use/find-and-open-device/).
- **Device page** — health, values, diagnostics, downlinks, and logs. See [The device page](/use/device-page/).
