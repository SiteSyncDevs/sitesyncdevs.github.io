## Launch in a browser

You may be given the project URL directly. Otherwise, launch it from the Ignition Gateway:

1. Open the Ignition Gateway and go to **Home**.
2. Open the **Perspective Session Launcher**.
3. Search for **Field**, then **Launch Project**.

:::note[Camera scanning is mobile-only]
QR-code camera scanning requires the [Perspective mobile app](/products/field-app/mobile-setup/). In the browser, add devices [manually](/use/add-device/add-manually/).
:::
