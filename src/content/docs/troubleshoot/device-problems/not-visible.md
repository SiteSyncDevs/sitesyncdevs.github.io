## What this means

A device isn't showing where you expect it.

## Most likely causes

- It has no **site** assigned — a device without a site won't appear in your fleet.
- You're filtered to a different site, sensor type, or status.
- It's assigned to a different site than expected.

## Check these first

1. Clear search/filters on the [Devices](/use/find-and-open-device/) list.
2. Confirm the device's assigned site.
3. Search by Dev EUI across sites.
