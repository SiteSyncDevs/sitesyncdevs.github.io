Naming conventions matter at scale. Agree on a format before you start adding sites — a consistent convention is far easier to search than ad hoc names.

A convention like `[Region]-[Building]-[Floor]` (for example, `EMEA-Berlin-B2`) works well.

Because devices are added to a folder that aligns with the site name, a clean naming scheme also keeps your Ignition tag structure tidy.
