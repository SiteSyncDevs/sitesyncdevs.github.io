## Summary

SiteSync 1.0.0 establishes a consistent operational model across the Enterprise dashboard, site dashboards, Field App, and supporting management views. It standardizes device health calculations, expands diagnostic context, improves filtering and navigation, strengthens device and connection management, and resolves a broad set of reliability and presentation issues.

The most significant changes are the new dual-margin signal-quality model, revised battery classifications, reconciled dashboard counts, an updated Site Health Score, and expanded diagnostic tooltips and problem-device messaging.

## Highlights

- Standardized device health calculations across Enterprise and Field App views.
- Introduced dual-margin signal ratings using both SNR margin and estimated signal power (ESP) margin.
- Added packet-delivery diagnostics based on dropped uplinks over a rolling 24-hour window.
- Updated battery health thresholds and aligned terminology across all dashboards and device views.
- Added contextual tooltips and plain-language diagnostic messaging.
- Overhauled Enterprise and site dashboard layouts, filtering, empty states, and refresh behavior.
- Refreshed device onboarding, bulk upload, QR Sync, network settings, and connection management.
- Improved mobile usability and removed desktop-only controls from Field App views.

## Health metric changes

See [Health metrics](/reference/health-metrics/) for full definitions. In summary:

- **Signal:** dual-margin rating (worse of SNR margin and ESP margin) — Strong ≥ 15 dB, Good 10–<15 dB, Fair 5–<10 dB, Poor < 5 dB.
- **Battery:** Good ≥ 3.5 V / ≥ 30%, Low (warning) 3.3–3.5 V / 15–30%, Critical < 3.3 V / < 15%. Volts or percent is auto-detected.
- **Status:** mutually exclusive — Online, Never Activated, Missed Check-in (formerly Offline), Decode Error, Other.
- **Site Health Score:** 50% uptime + 30% battery + 20% signal; never-activated devices excluded from uptime.

## Known issues

- Gateway-level health metrics are not shown on site or Enterprise dashboards.
- Never-activated timestamps, decoder-state logic, and very old Last Seen values need additional validation.
- Additional layout work remains for tag-path management in Standard projects.
