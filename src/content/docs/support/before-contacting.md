Before opening a ticket:

1. Check the relevant [troubleshooting](/troubleshoot/overview/) page for your symptom.
2. Run through the device [diagnostics workflow](/troubleshoot/device-diagnostics/) (Communication Flow).
3. Confirm your [connections](/configure/connections/health/) are Active.
4. Gather the details in [Collect a diagnostic package](/support/collect-diagnostic-package/).

Then [contact support](/support/contact-support/).
