## Purpose

Confirm a target SiteSync version is compatible with your environment before upgrading.

## Rules of thumb

- Keep the **SiteSync module** and its **Perspective projects on the same version** — when you upgrade, re-import all of the projects that ship with that version.
- Check the **minimum supported Ignition version** for the target release.
