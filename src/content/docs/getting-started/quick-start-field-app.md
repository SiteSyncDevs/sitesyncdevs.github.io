## Goal

Start onboarding and checking devices at your site.

## Steps

1. Open the Field App in a [browser](/products/field-app/browser-access/) or set up the [Perspective mobile app](/products/field-app/mobile-setup/).
2. [Select your site](/use/field-app/select-site/).
3. Add a device by [scanning its QR code](/use/add-device/scan-qr/) (mobile) or [entering details](/use/add-device/add-manually/).
4. [Validate activation](/use/add-device/validate-activation/), then [review device health](/use/field-app/review-health/).
