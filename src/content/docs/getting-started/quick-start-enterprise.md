## Goal

Stand up SiteSync Enterprise and get devices onboarded.

## Steps

1. Check [requirements](/install-upgrade/enterprise/requirements/).
2. [Install the module](/install-upgrade/install-module/), then import [Source + Enterprise Management](/install-upgrade/enterprise/import-enterprise-management/) and the [Field App](/install-upgrade/enterprise/import-field-app/).
3. [Configure identity and roles](/install-upgrade/enterprise/configure-identity-roles/).
4. Add your [connection(s)](/configure/connections/add-a-connection/) and create [sites](/configure/sites/create-a-site/).
5. Create [device profiles](/configure/device-profiles/create/), then onboard devices (QR or [bulk upload](/use/bulk-upload/download-template/)).
6. [Validate](/install-upgrade/enterprise/validate/) and watch the fleet dashboard.
