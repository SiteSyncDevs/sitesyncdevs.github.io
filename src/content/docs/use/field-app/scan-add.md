In the field, the fastest way to onboard a device is to scan its QR code in the Perspective mobile app — the join keys populate automatically. Full steps and supported formats are on [Scan a QR code](/use/add-device/scan-qr/).

1. Confirm you're in the right [site](/use/field-app/select-site/).
2. Go to **Devices → Add Device** and launch the scanner.
3. Scan the device label, confirm the device type, and submit.
4. [Validate activation](/use/add-device/validate-activation/).
