## Steps

1. [Open the device](/use/field-app/scan-find/).
2. Read the **Status** panel:
   - **Battery** — current level (`--` if not reported).
   - **Signal Score** — 0–100; below 50 may indicate connectivity issues.
   - **Device Temperature** — hardware temperature.
   - **Check-in Status** — "On Time" vs late/missed.
3. Check the **Alerts** panel — a healthy device shows **0 Active** alerts.

If something looks wrong, move on to [field diagnostics](/use/field-app/field-diagnostics/).
