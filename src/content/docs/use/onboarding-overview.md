SiteSync supports two ways to add devices: one at a time with **QR Sync**, or in large batches with **Bulk Upload**. Both require you to **select a site** first — a device without a site will not appear in your fleet.

| | QR Sync | Bulk Upload |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | One device at a time, field installs | Many devices at once, pre-deployment |
| How it works | Fill in a form or scan a QR code on the device | Upload a completed CSV file |
| Site required | Yes | Yes |
| Access from | **QR Sync** in the sidebar (best on mobile) | **Bulk Upload** in the sidebar |

## Choose a path

- Adding a device in the field, or just a few? Use [QR Sync](/use/add-device/scan-qr/) — or [add it manually](/use/add-device/add-manually/).
- Deploying many devices of the same type to one site? Use [Bulk Upload](/use/bulk-upload/download-template/).

After onboarding, [validate activation](/use/add-device/validate-activation/) to confirm the device joins and reports.
