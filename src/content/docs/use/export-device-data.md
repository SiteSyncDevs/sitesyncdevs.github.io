## Goal

Export your fleet list — filtered or complete — as a file.

## Steps

1. Open **Devices** (Device Management).
2. Optionally apply a search or filters to narrow the table.
3. Select the **download icon** above the table (to the left of the **Columns** button).

The export reflects the **current view** — if filters or a search term are active, only the matching devices are included.

Use it to generate a fleet report, share a filtered list, or import device data into another system.
